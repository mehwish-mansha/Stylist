Booking & Scheduling

Visit our Contact page or click "Book an Appointment" from anywhere on the site. Select your preferred service, choose a stylist, and pick an available time slot. You'll receive a confirmation email within 24 hours.

We recommend booking at least 3–5 days in advance to ensure your preferred stylist is available. For bridal and event packages, we suggest booking 4–6 weeks ahead. Same-week bookings are sometimes possible — contact us directly.

Yes. We have stylists available Monday through Saturday, 10 AM to 8 PM. Evening slots (after 6 PM) are limited — we recommend booking these early in the week to secure your spot.

Services

Personal Styling focuses on building new outfits and curating a lookbook of pieces for you to acquire. Wardrobe Analysis starts with what you already own — sorting, editing, and identifying gaps before making any new purchases. Many clients do both in sequence.

Session duration depends on the package. Essential sessions run 60 minutes. Signature sessions are 90–120 minutes. Full wardrobe edits can run 3–4 hours. All durations are outlined clearly on each service page before you book.

A digital lookbook is a beautifully formatted PDF your stylist creates after your session. It contains curated outfit combinations, where to buy each piece (with links), fabric and care notes, and styling tips. It's yours to keep and reference whenever you get dressed.

Not necessarily. We always begin by maximising what you already own. New pieces are only suggested when there's a genuine gap. You're never pressured to buy anything — our job is to help you feel great in what works for your life and budget.

Overwhelmingly, yes. Our virtual sessions are rated equally as highly as in-person ones. The key is good lighting for the video call and having your wardrobe accessible. We've refined our virtual process over hundreds of sessions and it works beautifully.

Payments & Refunds

We accept Visa, Mastercard, JazzCash, EasyPaisa, and direct bank transfer. All card payments are processed securely through our payment partners. We do not store full card numbers on our servers.

Instalment options are available on our Prestige and Edit+Style packages. Contact us before booking and we'll arrange a suitable payment plan. A 50% deposit is required to confirm any booking on instalment.

Cancellations made 48+ hours before a session receive a full refund. Cancellations 24–48 hours before receive a 50% refund plus 50% platform credit. Less than 24 hours: no refund, but one free reschedule is offered. Full details are in our Terms of Service.

All listed prices are inclusive of applicable sales tax. The final amount you see at checkout is the amount you pay — there are no hidden fees or surcharges added during payment.

Receipts are sent automatically to your registered email within 30 minutes of payment. Check your spam folder first. If you still can't find it, email hello@stylist.pk with your name and booking date and we'll resend it promptly.

Account & Privacy

Click the user icon in the top navigation bar to be taken to the login/registration page. Creating an account takes under 2 minutes and only requires your name, email, and a password.

Yes. Your style profile, wardrobe photos, and body data are only shared with your assigned stylist and are never used for advertising or sold to third parties. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

You can request account deletion by emailing hello@stylist.pk from your registered address with the subject "Account Deletion Request". We'll process it within 30 days. Financial records are retained for 7 years as required by law.

Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and enter your registered email address. You'll receive a reset link within 5 minutes. If it doesn't arrive, check your spam folder or contact support.

Collections & Products

Our collections showcase curated pieces from our partner designers and boutiques. For purchase, you'll be directed to the relevant brand's website or boutique. Our Shopping Service can also handle the purchasing process on your behalf.

We update our collections seasonally — typically four major updates per year aligned with Eid, Summer, Wedding Season, and Winter. New arrivals from partner brands are added on a rolling basis throughout the year.

The Compare tool lets you view two products side by side — comparing fabric, price, sizing, stylist recommendations, and more. Access it from any product card or visit the Compare page directly.

Click the heart icon on any product card to save it to your Favourites. Access all saved items via the heart icon in the top navigation. You'll need to be logged in for your favourites to persist across sessions.
