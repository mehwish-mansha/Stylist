Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 2026

01 Information We Collect

To provide our bespoke styling services, we collect information that helps us understand your unique aesthetic and needs:

  • Personal Details: Name, contact information, and shipping address.
  • Style Profile: Body measurements, sizing, brand preferences, and lifestyle data.
  • Digital Footprint: Cookies and tracking technologies to improve your browsing experience.

02 How Your Data Is Used

Your privacy is our priority. We use your data exclusively to enhance your experience:

  • Curating personalized digital lookbooks and shopping suggestions.
  • Facilitating appointments with your chosen stylist.
  • Analyzing trends to improve our selection of couture and bespoke pieces.

03 Data Security

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. Your style profile is encrypted and only accessible by authorized stylists and concierge staff during your session periods.

04 Your Rights

You have full control over your data. At any time, you may request to view, edit, or permanently delete your style profile and account history from our database.
